YOUR EXISTING RUNELITE. AN OLDER ADVENTURE.

The journey starts
inside RuneLite.

2005Scape is a plugin for your usual RuneLite setup. Your account, your client, your historical challenge.

Not yet available in Plugin Hub. The plugin is being prepared for review. Public source, in-game validation and RuneLite approval are required. Downloading a JAR does not install it into ordinary RuneLite.
AFTER PLUGIN HUB APPROVAL

Three steps to another era.

01 / OPEN

Your usual RuneLite.

Use your existing RuneLite client and open its plugin settings.

02 / INSTALL

Find 2005Scape.

Open Plugin Hub, search for 2005Scape and install it. This step becomes available only after approval.

03 / BEGIN

Set your challenge.

Enable the plugin. Starting possessions become the baseline. New acquisitions earn progression.

CHECK BEFORE YOU CHASE

Know your historical boundaries.

The cutoff is 22 June 2005 inclusive. Unknown item variants are blocked by default; unknown new receipt sources require review. The plugin limits the modern game but cannot restore old server mechanics.
